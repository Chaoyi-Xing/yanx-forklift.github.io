Frequently Asked Questions

Still deciding? Contact sales@yanx-forklift.com and tell us about your application.

Which type of forklift or warehouse truck should I choose?

The right choice depends on load weight, lift height, aisle width, travel distance, working hours and the way goods are stored.

  • Electric forklift: general pallet handling with a counterbalance design.
  • VNA forklift: very narrow aisles and high-density warehouse storage.
  • Reach truck: high-rack pallet storage with compact aisle operation.
  • Stacker truck: electric, semi-electric or manual lifting and stacking in warehouses and workshops.
  • Pallet truck: horizontal pallet movement over short distances.
  • Manual equipment: light-duty handling where simple operation is preferred.
What information is needed for a quotation?

Please provide the required lifting capacity, maximum lift height, aisle width, truck type, battery preference, operating environment, quantity and destination port or city. Photos of the working area are also useful.

Can you provide OEM branding or custom configurations?

OEM color, logo and selected configuration options may be available depending on model and order quantity. Tell us your target market and requirements so we can confirm what is practical.

Do you provide spare parts and after-sales support?

Yes. We can help identify common replacement parts based on the truck model, serial information and photos. We also provide remote communication for setup and common technical questions.

How are forklifts packed and shipped?

Shipping plans depend on truck size, quantity and destination. Equipment is prepared for export transport, secured appropriately and coordinated with the selected sea or land freight method.
