Product Overview

The CDS1030 and CDS1530 form a semi-electric straddle stacker series for pallet lifting, stacking and warehouse material handling. The range covers rated capacities of 1,000 kg and 1,500 kg with a 3,000 mm standard lift height.

The straddle-leg structure supports a wider range of pallet configurations, while the compact 800 mm overall width and 1,450 mm turning radius assist operation in limited warehouse spaces. Final travel, battery and mast configuration should be confirmed according to the customer's operating requirements.
